Invoice Approval Policy

1. Automatic Approval
Invoices of ₹50,000 or less may be processed automatically
when all invoice validation requirements are satisfied.

2. Manager Approval
Invoices above ₹50,000 require manager approval before
the invoice can be processed.

3. Exception Approval
Manager approval is also required when:
- Invoice details conflict with the purchase order.
- Vendor information cannot be verified.
- Required information is missing.
- The agent detects a policy exception.

4. Human Decision
The agent must stop before performing the restricted action
and request human approval.

The agent must clearly explain:
- Why approval is required.
- What information was verified.
- What action is waiting for approval.

5. After Approval
Once approval is received, the agent may continue the
remaining permitted steps and must verify the final result.
