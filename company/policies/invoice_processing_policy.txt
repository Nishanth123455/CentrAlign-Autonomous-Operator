Invoice Processing Policy

1. Invoice Validation
- Every invoice must have a valid invoice number.
- The vendor must exist in the approved vendor records.
- The invoice amount must be checked against the related purchase order.
- The invoice must not already be marked as paid.

2. Automatic Processing
An invoice may be processed automatically when:
- The invoice matches an approved purchase order.
- The invoice amount matches the purchase order amount.
- The vendor is approved.
- The invoice is not already paid.
- The invoice amount is ₹50,000 or less.

3. Human Approval
Human approval is required when:
- The invoice amount is greater than ₹50,000.
- The invoice amount does not match the purchase order.
- The vendor cannot be verified.
- The agent finds conflicting information.

4. Verification
An invoice must not be considered completed merely because
an action was submitted.

The system must verify that the invoice status in the
company system reflects the intended final state.

5. Evidence
The completed task should provide:
- Invoice number
- Vendor
- Amount
- Final status
- Relevant policy used
- Actions performed
- Verification result
